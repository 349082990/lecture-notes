Immersive overlay

The small, frameless reader you switch to with . It shows a few lines of your document at a time and floats over other apps.

Lines shown at a timeHow much of the document the overlay shows at once.
Custom number of lineslines (1–25)
Lines per scrollHow far one scroll moves when 3 or more lines are shown. 1- and 2-line views always move to the next line(s).
Headings"Same size as text" keeps the overlay compact — headings keep their bold, italics and colour. Jump between headings with / .
Text size Bigger or smaller text in the overlay only (Ctrl + scroll over it).
MarginsSpace between the text and the edge of the overlay.
Overlay opacity See-through-ness of the whole overlay, text included. steps through 100 → 80 → 60 → 40 → 20%. The editor is never see-through.
Overlay always on topKeeps the immersive overlay above other windows. Immersive mode only — the editor, including full screen, is a normal window that other apps can go in front of.
Background opacity Only the overlay's background — lower it to see what's behind while the text stays solid.
